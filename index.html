About us

A traditional pub for a new generation

Cask & Barrel opened on Yarm High Street in 2025 in the former Arabian Nights building at Winpenny House. We’re all about a rotating selection of real ales, alongside wines and whiskies you won’t find elsewhere on the street.

Think good conversation, friendly staff and a relaxed atmosphere. Come in for a quiet afternoon pint or an evening with friends.
