Headline features

Three things that earn their keep

Editable beer board

Change a beer from your phone in 30 seconds – no web designer needed. Update a simple Google Sheet and the “On the bar now” board refreshes, with ABV, style and a “coming soon” row.

Christmas bookings

A seasonal banner and a short enquiry form for parties, wakes and tasting evenings, so enquiries arrive in your inbox ready to answer.

Open now + map

“Open now – until 10pm” worked out from your real hours, plus a map and one-tap directions on phones, where most customers will look.
